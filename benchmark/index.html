Method and sources

How this report was put together

Prices are list prices, collected on one day

Every price was taken from the provider's own pricing page on October 13, 2026: pay-as-you-go packs and subscriptions separately, excluding tax and coupon codes. Where a provider shows a standing sale price that every buyer is charged, that price is used and the struck-through list price is recorded in the data file. Third-party review sites were not used as a source for any number.

Normalised to 10,000 verifications

Where a provider sells a pack of exactly 10,000 credits, that price is used. Where it does not, the price is prorated from the smallest pay-as-you-go pack that covers 10,000 and marked with an asterisk. Providers that sell subscriptions only are listed but not ranked, rather than converted into a pay-as-you-go rate they do not offer.

No accuracy or latency league table

Mail servers rate-limit, greylist, and give false answers to unfamiliar senders, so no email verifier can honour one fixed accuracy figure across every domain, and response times vary with the receiving server. This edition therefore compares terms that can be checked: price, what is billed, what expires, and what is free. To compare accuracy, run 1,000 of your own addresses through two or three shortlisted providers and check the results against delivery outcomes you can confirm.

What this report does not cover

Deliverability and inbox placement, provider infrastructure and uptime, and data-protection or compliance review. Each needs its own assessment against your requirements.

FAQs

Email checker and email verification FAQs

What is an email checker?

An email checker tells you whether an email address can receive mail before you send to it. A full check covers the format, the domain and its MX records, then asks the receiving mail server about the mailbox and stops before any message is sent. Try the BillionVerify email checker.

How do I check if an email is valid?

Paste the address into an email checker or call an email verification API. BillionVerify returns one status per address: valid, invalid, catch-all, role, disposable or unknown, with the reason behind it. Check if an email is valid.

What is the difference between email verification and email validation?

Email validation checks that an address is written correctly: syntax, domain and MX records. Email verification goes one step further and asks the mail server whether the mailbox exists. BillionVerify does both in one request. See email validation and email verification.

How much does an email verification service cost?

In this report, 10,000 pay-as-you-go checks cost from $11.90 to $129 across the 15 providers with public packs. BillionVerify works out to $10 per 10,000, with packs from $20 for 20,000 credits, and goes down to $0.00014 per email on annual plans. See pricing.

Is there a free email verifier?

Yes. The BillionVerify free email checker runs 20 full checks every 24 hours without an account. A free account adds 20 free credits every day you log in, up to 600 a month, with no credit card, and the credits never expire.

How do I verify an email address without sending an email?

An email verifier opens an SMTP conversation with the receiving mail server, asks about the mailbox, and disconnects before any message is sent, so the person behind the address receives nothing. Verify an email address.

How do I choose an email verification tool?

Compare more than the headline price: whether unknown results are billed, whether credits expire, how much you can test for free, and whether there is an official API and MCP server. The tables in this report answer each point for 19 providers. See email verification tools compared.

Can I run bulk email verification on a large list?

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file with no row limit. BillionVerify verifies every row in the background, notifies you by email or webhook, and lets you download the results filtered by status with your original columns kept. See bulk email verification.

How often should I run email list verification?

Email lists decay by about 22.5% a year as people change jobs and abandon inboxes, so verify a list before every major campaign and at least once a quarter. Checking the same address again within 24 hours is free. See email list cleaning.

Do I pay for unknown or catch-all results?

Unknown results are free on BillionVerify: if a mail server will not give a definite answer, you are not charged. Catch-all is a definite result and costs 1 credit, but it is reported as its own status and never counted as valid. 12 of the other 18 providers also say unknown is free.

Can AI agents use an email verification API?

Yes. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another MCP client to the hosted BillionVerify MCP server and sign in with OAuth, or call the email verification API with an API key. Single checks and batches of up to 50 addresses are supported.
