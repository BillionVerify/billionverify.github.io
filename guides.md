# Guides

Canonical page: https://billionverify.github.io/guides/

- [Temp Mail in Plain Words: A Short Guide](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/temp-mail/): A free inbox with no sign-up that disappears on a timer. How it works, where it fails and why sites block it.
- [10 Minute Mail: How Timed Inboxes Differ](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/10-minute-mail/): 10 Minute Mail deletes your address after ten minutes. See how other timed inboxes compare on lifespan and privacy.
- [YOPmail, Guerrilla Mail and Mailinator Side by Side](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/yopmail-guerrilla-mail-mailinator/): Three of the oldest free inbox sites, compared on privacy, retention and sending, from their own pages.
- [Temporary Phone Numbers: What They Can and Cannot Do](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/temporary-phone-number/): How shared and private virtual numbers receive SMS codes, and why many websites refuse them.
- [How to Block Temporary Email on a Signup Form](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/block-temporary-email/): Domain lists, mail hosts and signup patterns: the signals that flag a throwaway address, and how to use them.
- [Email Verification in Node.js and Python](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/email-verification-node-python/): Server-side API calls, retries, caching and batch jobs for email verification in Node.js and Python.
- [Email Validation API: How It Works and How to Use It](https://billionverify.github.io/guides/email-validation-api/): The checks behind an email validation API, what each status means and when to run real-time or bulk checks.
